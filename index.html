Two rules

No stock markup is carried over — the rewrite is genuine, not a reskin. And every existing feature must survive it.

舊 markup 一件都唔留,係真重寫唔係換膚。但係功能一個都唔准少。

Honest reporting

A build is not called green until it is green. A feature is not called preserved until it is wired. Styling a surface is not the same as keeping it working.

未綠就唔叫綠,未駁通就唔叫保住咗。畫靚咗個樣唔等於功能仲喺度。
